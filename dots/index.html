What it is

OpenAI's agent for ongoing projects between chats.

Setup

  1. Check the access guide. Pro excludes the EEA, Switzerland and UK. Business Premium covers supported ChatGPT regions. Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare need admin-enabled beta access. Rollout may take several days.

  2. Open ChatGPT's desktop app or desktop browser and follow setup. Name your dot and choose a character. You cannot create one on mobile.

  3. Connect only the apps your project needs and review their permissions. Local computer access is optional and starts off.

  4. Give it a concrete goal and define what it can do independently. Review progress in its profile and check consequential work.

Cheat sheet

  • Official announcement.
  • Privacy, permissions and approval controls.
  • Use Custom Rules to set boundaries. Dots can make mistakes; review important results.
